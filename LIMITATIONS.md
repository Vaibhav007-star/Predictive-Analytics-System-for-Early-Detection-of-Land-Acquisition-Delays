# Limitations & Boundary Conditions: SIH26017

**System:** Predictive Analytics System for Early Detection of Land Acquisition Delays  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India  
**Document Classification:** Mandatory Integrity & Transparency Disclosure

---

## 1. Data Coverage & Scope Limitations

1. **Central Sector Threshold:**
   The primary dataset comprises infrastructure assets monitored by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) costing **₹150 Crore and above** ($N = 1,477$ ongoing projects as of February 2025). Smaller state-funded public works or private infrastructure initiatives fall outside this monitoring framework unless captured under rural road schemes like PMGSY.
2. **Exclusion of Sub-Judice Cadastral Dockets:**
   Open government bulk publications do not provide parcel-level landowner identities, survey numbers, or real-time district court / High Court case dockets. Consequently, land acquisition bottleneck severity is modeled through empirical agency and state historical delay tendencies, the RFCTLARR Act 2013 regulatory regime shift, linear corridor characteristics, and project duration/cost scale rather than individual litigation filings.

---

## 2. Geospatial Precision Disclosures

1. **State Administrative Centroid Mapping:**
   Central sector infrastructure projects published in MoSPI reports specify the executing State or Union Territory (e.g. "MAHARASHTRA", "UTTAR PRADESH", or "MULTI STATE"). Precise sub-meter GPS coordinates for entire 300 km highway corridors or railway lines are not published in the monthly Flash Report tables.
2. **Strict Non-Fabrication Protocol:**
   In compliance with our ethical non-fabrication directive, **we do not generate synthetic latitude and longitude coordinates**. All map markers on the GIS dashboard plot at verified State administrative centroids with explicit disclosure of `geo_precision: "State-level centroid (MoSPI official location)"`. Users and judges should not interpret centroid markers as point-source construction sites.

---

## 3. Machine Learning & Predictive Boundaries

1. **Correlation vs. Causation:**
   Feature attributions derived from SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations) represent **conditional risk associations** based on historical trends. An elevated risk score does *not* constitute proof of contractor negligence, corrupt practices, or administrative fault.
2. **Right-Censored Ongoing Projects:**
   Delay status is defined relative to the February 2025 reporting milestone. Projects sanctioned recently (e.g. late 2023 or 2024) that have not yet missed their original target commissioning date are categorized as on-time ($y = 0$). Although feature representations account for early project gestation, future delays may emerge as civil execution progresses.
3. **Macroeconomic & External Shocks:**
   The model does not anticipate unforeseen force majeure events (e.g., severe localized flooding, seismic disruptions, geopolitical supply-chain embargoes) that occur subsequent to project sanction date.

---

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop Governance Requirement

The predictions, scores, and recommended actions generated by this system are **advisory decision-support tools** designed to prioritize administrative attention and expedite inter-ministerial coordination. Under no circumstances should this system be utilized to:
- Automatically revoke project sanctions;
- Impose liquidated damages on executing agencies without independent human inquiry;
- Displace statutory social impact assessments or fair compensation hearings under Section 15 of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.
